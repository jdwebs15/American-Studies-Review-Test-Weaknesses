Game Overview

This review game targets the Ohio American Government standards tied to the Northwest Ordinance, the structure and principles of the U.S. Constitution, and the Federalist / Anti-Federalist debate that led to the Bill of Rights.

Students Will Practice

  • Precedents established by the Northwest Ordinance
  • Popular sovereignty, limited government, and federalism
  • Separation of powers and checks and balances
  • Constitutional structure and powers of government
  • Federalist and Anti-Federalist arguments
  • How debate led to the Bill of Rights

Build Features

  • Question order shuffles on each new run
  • Answer choices shuffle each round
  • Hint on miss without giving away the answer
  • Students must correct before moving on
  • Auto-advance on correct response
  • Restart button included
